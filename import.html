Import from Arc

Bring your Arc spaces, folders and pinned tabs into Sidecar.

  1. In Arc, open the Arc menu → Settings → Advanced → Export Data. Arc saves a Markdown (.md) file.
  2. Choose that file here, or drop it on this box.

Pick a Sidecar space for each Arc space, then tick the links to bring over. Links already in that Sidecar space start unticked. Arc doesn't export page titles, so imported tabs show the site's name until you first open them.

Changed your mind? Click Undo in the sidebar (or press ⌘Z there) to take the whole import back.
